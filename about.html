02 / Background

About.

My focus is the overlap between cybersecurity and intelligence: understanding technical evidence, evaluating information, identifying meaningful patterns, and communicating what matters.

Austin, Texas

CG

I’m building toward cybersecurity and intelligence-analysis work, with a particular interest in security operations and cyber threat intelligence.

I study Computing Applications at Texas Tech University and serve in the U.S. Army Reserve as an Intelligence Analyst. The intelligence side of my background has trained me to organize information, evaluate reliability, identify gaps, develop supported judgments, and communicate findings clearly.

My technical projects add the cybersecurity layer. I’m working with authentication logs, packet captures, DNS investigations, SIEM concepts, KQL, incident documentation, and structured investigative workflows. The goal is not to collect unrelated tools. It is to get better at turning technical evidence into useful analysis.

The projects on this site use fictional, non-sensitive, or openly available information. They are designed to demonstrate process, documentation, reasoning, and communication without representing operational or classified work.

02 / Approach

Technical evidence first.

I want each project to answer the same questions: What happened? What evidence supports it? How reliable is that evidence? What can be concluded? What remains unknown? And how should the finding be communicated to someone making a decision?
