# Carlos Gomez | Personal Portfolio

## What is this?

This is my portfolio for cybersecurity, intelligence analysis, and all my TTU technical coursework. The site has Home, Projects, About, and Resume pages and a case study for my completed 5K Preparation Knowledge Base, and a Project Plans page for four labs and analytical products I intend to build. Visitors can filter projects by skill area and save a light or dark theme preference. All my planned work is labeled Coming Soon so it cannot be mistaken for completed experience.

## Why does it exist?

I wanted one place where an instructor, employer, or colleague could understand my background and inspect work I have actually completed. My Computing Applications coursework and Army Reserve intelligence training have led me toward security and analytical roles. The completed case study explains decisions and process, while the project plans give a clear account of what I intend to demonstrate next im hoping will attract employers

## What tools did I use?

- **VS Code:** I edit the site's individual pages, styles, and scripts in a familiar development environment.
- **HTML and CSS:** Semantic pages and custom responsive styles give me control over the structure and design without a framework.
- **Vanilla JavaScript:** It runs the skill filter, remembers the theme, marks the current navigation item, and reveals sections as they enter view.
- **Git and GitHub:** I track changes and maintain the public source repository.
- **GitHub Pages:** It publishes this static site from the repository after a push.
- **ChatGPT/Codex:** I used it for drafting code, troubleshooting, and reviewing wording, then made the final content and design decisions and checked the result.

The portrait and three graphics on the site were supplied for this project. The small graphics identify security, intelligence, and Texas Tech rather than standing in for screenshots of unfinished labs.
