Authentication Log Triage

Coming Soon

I want to examine sign-in activity and decide which patterns deserve further investigation.

Plan
Use a safe, synthetic set of authentication events. Compare failures, successful logins, account names, times, and source addresses to distinguish ordinary mistakes from unusual sequences. Record the rule behind every finding.
What I will show
A documented dataset, queries or spreadsheet analysis, a short triage log, and a conclusion that separates evidence from assumptions.

Microsoft Sentinel Mini SOC

Coming Soon

This lab will show how a small security monitoring workflow can move from an event to a documented decision.

Plan
Set up an authorized practice environment, ingest sample security events, write and explain KQL queries, create a simple detection, and walk through alert triage. I will keep lab data separate from any real account or organization.
What I will show
An environment diagram, redacted screenshots, KQL examples, a detection rationale, an investigation timeline, and a brief incident summary. Cloud setup and cost will determine the exact lab scope.

Network security

Wireshark DNS Investigation

Coming Soon

I plan to troubleshoot a controlled DNS problem by following the packets rather than guessing at the cause.

Plan
Capture traffic from my own lab or use a permitted sample PCAP, filter DNS requests and responses, identify failed lookups or unexpected answers, and test an explanation against the packet evidence.
What I will show
Display filters, annotated packet screenshots, a short sequence of events, a conclusion, and the steps another person could use to repeat the analysis.

Intelligence analysis

Incident Brief Workbench

Coming Soon

I want to demonstrate how I turn scattered public information into a brief that someone can actually use.

Plan
Choose a public, unclassified cyber incident; collect reliable open sources; build a timeline; distinguish confirmed facts from reporting and interpretation; and state confidence and unanswered questions.
What I will show
A source table, incident timeline, concise written brief, citations, and a note explaining where the evidence is limited. No classified or private operational information will be used.
